The LONELY-EU database lists the European surveys that measure loneliness and feeds the [EU Loneliness Explorer](https://sppqgn-arturo-bertero.shinyapps.io/Lon_Shiny/). This document sets out how the database is updated every year. Each update searches the latest publication year under the protocol of Tang et al. (2025), corrects existing rows from their codebooks, and audits every cell before release.

Calibrated classifiers and language models screen, code and audit under written rules. Scripts check every output against the source documents. Every changed cell is logged with the evidence for the change. The first application, to the publication year 2025, produced release 2.0 of the database, with 359 surveys.

The code, briefs and coding manual are on [GitHub](https://github.com/arturobertero/LONELY-EU_D3.1).
