The paper defines statistical belief network models and compares six of them (correlation matrices, Belief Network Analysis, partial correlation networks, Correlational Class Analysis, Response-Item Networks, and Conceptual Similarity Judgments) on the 2024 American National Election Study and an original similarity-judgment sample, matching each method to the research question it answers.
